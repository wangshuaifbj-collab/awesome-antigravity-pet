# Categories

[简体中文](../zh-CN/categories.md) | English

`categories.json` is the source of truth for the repository taxonomy. Every pet keeps one focused `primary_category`; franchise, visual style, source type, and license status belong in tags and submission metadata.

## Game Characters

Characters originating from video games and game franchises.

## Anime Characters

Characters originating from anime, manga, light novels, and animation. Game-origin characters belong in Game Characters even when rendered in an anime style.

## Original Characters

Creator-owned characters with an original identity and design. Use Mascots when the character primarily represents a brand, product, community, or personal identity.

## Mascots

Friendly brand, product, community, and personal mascot companions.

## Animals

Pets based primarily on real-world animals and household companions.

## Fantasy Creatures

Magical creatures, monsters, hybrids, and supernatural companions.

## Robots

Mechanical, robotic, AI, and coding-oriented companions.

## Human Avatars

Real-person likenesses, creator avatars, and profile companions. These require a clear likeness or permission trail and must not be political campaign or propaganda content.

## Memes

Internet-native meme characters, reactions, and viral icons.

## Objects & Props

Animated objects, tools, food, vehicles, and prop-like companions that are not primarily a character, animal, or robot.

## Others

Temporary moderation fallback only. Use a more specific category whenever one fits; this category is hidden from normal discovery while empty.

## Duplicate control

The same canonical character or concept should not be collected repeatedly just because the author is different. A second variant needs a materially different design or runtime treatment and explicit maintainer approval. Generic human avatars and visually interchangeable mascot silhouettes can also be declined even when their names differ.
